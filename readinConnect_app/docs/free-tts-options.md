# Free TTS Options for ReadingConnect

## 1. Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (BEST FREE OPTION)
**Free Tier: 4 million characters/month**

- WaveNet voices included in free tier
- Natural, high-quality speech
- No credit card required for free tier
- Easy Firebase integration

**Setup:**
1. Create Google Cloud account
2. Enable Text-to-Speech API
3. Get API key
4. 4M chars/month = ~60,000 words/month FREE

---

## 2. Amazon Polly (AWS)
**Free Tier: 5 million characters/month for 12 months**

- Neural voices available
- Very natural sounding
- Free for first year
- Then ~$4 per million characters

---

## 3. Microsoft Azure Speech
**Free Tier: 0.5 million characters/month**

- Neural voices
- Good quality
- Limited free tier

---

## 4. ElevenLabs
**Free Tier: 10,000 characters/month**

- Extremely natural AI voices
- Best quality available
- Very limited free tier (good for testing)
- $5/month for 30,000 chars

---

## 5. ResponsiveVoice (FREE)
**Completely Free**

- No API key needed for basic use
- Better than browser voices
- Not as good as Google/Amazon
- Good intermediate solution

---

## 6. Pre-Recorded Audio (100% FREE)
**One-time recording effort**

- Record yourself or hire voice actor once
- Use for core vocabulary
- Works offline forever
- Best quality possible

**Recommended recording list:**
- 26 alphabet letters
- 50 most common CVC words (cat, hat, mat, etc.)
- 20 blends (bl, cl, fl, etc.)
- Basic instructions

**Tools:**
- Phone voice recorder
- Audacity (free software)
- Or use ElevenLabs free tier to generate

---
